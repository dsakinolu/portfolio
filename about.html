About Me

Hi! My legal name is Deborah O. Akin-Olukunle, but I go by Sefunmi. I’m a fourth-year Informatics student at Indiana University Bloomington, passionate about combining technology, healthcare, and education to make a meaningful impact.

My journey began as a pre-med student, aspiring to become an Obstetrician & Gynecologist. Along the way, I discovered the transformative power of technology to improve access, learning, and healthcare — especially for children and underserved communities. This realization led me to Informatics, where I could combine my analytical skills, creativity, and problem-solving to build tools that truly make a difference.

Through my studies, I’ve developed expertise in data science, web development, and African languages. I love applying these skills to real-world projects. For instance, I independently designed the Yorùbá Learning Website, an interactive platform that teaches children the Yorùbá language through games, music, and engaging content. I also built a web-based e-commerce application and a dynamic data interface using MySQL and PHP — projects that combined backend logic, intuitive design, and data visualization.

Beyond academics, I thrive in leadership and community roles. I founded and manage C.H. Café at RCCG Covenant House Indianapolis, where I coordinate volunteers, optimize operations, design branding materials, and create welcoming spaces for the community. My experiences as a research assistant, teaching intern, and equity-focused program intern have reinforced my love for mentorship, learning, and tech-driven problem solving.

My goal is to apply my skills to healthcare and educational initiatives that positively impact children and communities. Whether through interactive apps, robotics, or data-driven solutions, I aim to create experiences that are innovative, accessible, and meaningful.
