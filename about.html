About me

Hi! My legal name is Deborah O. Akin-Olukunle, but I go by Sefunmi. I'm a data scientist and web developer with a B.Sc. in Informatics with Distinction from Indiana University Bloomington, with minors in Data Science, Web Design & Development, and African Languages — now completing my M.Sc. in Data Science at IU.

My journey began as a pre-med student aspiring to become an Obstetrician & Gynecologist. Along the way I discovered the transformative power of technology to improve access, learning, and healthcare — especially for children and underserved communities. That realization led me to Informatics, where I could combine analytical skill, creativity, and problem-solving to build tools that make a real difference.

Through my studies I've developed expertise in data science, web development, and African languages. I independently designed the Yorùbá Learning Website, an interactive platform that teaches children Yorùbá through games and music, and built full-stack database applications with PHP and MySQL.

As a Product Owner and Software QA Engineer Intern at iGeeksNG Solutions, I work on quality assurance testing for BizAppointly, an appointment and business scheduling platform — writing and running test plans, reporting defects, and translating what users need into development priorities.

Beyond academics, I thrive in leadership and community roles. I founded and manage C.H. Café at RCCG Covenant House Indianapolis, where I coordinate volunteers, optimize operations with data, design branding, and create a welcoming space for the community.

My goal is to apply data and technology to healthcare and educational initiatives that positively impact children and communities — through interactive apps, dashboards, and data-driven solutions that are innovative, accessible, and meaningful.
